The Fear & Greed Index hit 18 this week.

EXTREME FEAR.

I'm watching this from inside a bank. And I need to tell you what it actually looks like from this side.

When clients call asking if they should sell everything — these are the same people who six months ago told me they "believe in the long term." Same people. Different emotion. Same fundamentals.

Here's what I notice from inside the institution: the building doesn't shake. The screens are red, the index is screaming, and the institutional response is... measured. The models still run. The meetings still happen.

The only thing that changes is the phone rings more.

There's a gap between how scared the market FEELS and how calm the institution STAYS. That gap is the signal — not the number.

Think of it like a raid boss fight. When the boss enrages and everyone's health bars are dropping — the experienced players get calm. They've seen this mechanic. They know the rotation.

EXTREME FEAR at 18 means the new players are mashing buttons.

I don't know where the market goes from here. Nobody does. But I know what fear looks like when you're managing the money — and it looks very different from this side of the glass.

The gap. Pay attention to the gap.
