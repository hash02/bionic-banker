Definition

What is risk evidence?

Risk evidence is the set of records a reader can check before trusting a risk profile: transaction activity, sanctions or watchlist context, fraud signals, timestamps, source notes, and the limits of what the evidence can prove. It is not a final legal, compliance, or trading decision.

On Bionic Banker, a risk profile should help a non-technical reader see what happened, why it matters, what remains uncertain, and which source records deserve a second look.

Reader checklist

What to verify before using a risk profile

A useful profile should make the source trail visible enough for a reader to check it. Before relying on one, look for these four items.

  • Source records: links, timestamps, or public records behind the claim.
  • Evidence limits: a clear statement of what the records do not prove.
  • Decision boundary: no legal, compliance, trading, tax, or investment instruction.
  • Next review step: what a human analyst, builder, or reader should inspect next.
