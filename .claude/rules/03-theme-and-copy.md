# Theme and Copy Rules

## Visual theme

The locked Bionic Banker theme is serious finance/compliance plus calm field-map/game-board framing.

Use:

- green: `#1fa068` and transparent `rgba(31,160,104,...)`
- near-black: `#010302`, `#031008`, `#06170d`, `#08080e` where already established
- white/off-white: `#f7fff9`, `#e4e4ec`, `#def8e5`
- muted green-grey for secondary text

Avoid unless Hash explicitly requests a new design system:

- blue/cyan accents such as `#67e8f9`, `#6fe7ff`, `rgba(103,232,249,...)`, `rgba(111,231,255,...)`
- purple/red/rainbow gradients
- loud cyberpunk neon styling
- grey/blue mobile canvas overlays

## Homepage and field-map vocabulary

Preferred labels:

- Start Quest
- Mission Map
- Field Pack
- Health Board
- Apps
- Signals
- AI Intelligence
- Blog
- About
- mission, stop, route, next move

Keep the tone grown-up. This is not childish gamification. It should feel like a calm reviewer map for serious finance systems.

## Persona naming

Use `Agent Her` when referring to the assistant/persona as a visible named collaborator. Do not blindly replace lowercase pronouns.

## Copy standards

Good public copy is short, specific, and bounded:

- what exists
- what it proves
- what evidence backs it
- what it cannot do
- who should inspect it

Avoid inflated phrasing:

- revolutionary
- fully autonomous
- guaranteed
- production-grade unless production-grade checks exist
- bank/company-negative framing

## Dash and glyph discipline

For the public conversion surfaces, avoid long dash glyphs `—` and `–`. Use commas, periods, colons, or short hyphens where needed.
