Evidence note

Analyst-readable record

Observed

Transactions show high scores due to multiple rule hits, especially concentrated inflow and burst behavior.

Evidence available

Sender/receiver, amount, timestamp, country/context marker, rule-hit reasons, alert flag.

Evidence missing

Off-chain identity, customer profile, stated business purpose, source-of-funds documentation, verified entity attribution.

Allowed next step

Escalate to human analyst for source review and false-positive check.

Not allowed

No filing, freezing, accusation, wallet action, or customer decision from this dashboard alone.

Human review decision box

Recommended action: escalate for review, not enforcement.

The correct system behavior is to preserve the source trail, list missing evidence, and ask a human analyst to review. It should not claim criminality or trigger autonomous action.

Buttons are intentionally disabled in the public demo. This is a proof surface, not an operational compliance system.
