# AML Review Engine Demo Pack — Limitations

- Research and demonstration material only.
- Not legal, regulatory, tax, compliance, trading, investment, KYC, or law-enforcement advice.
- Public chain signals do not prove private identity or intent by themselves.
- A risk score is a triage signal, not a final verdict.
- The demo does not file reports, freeze funds, approve customers, or move assets.
- Any production use would need governance, validation, data-quality review, access control, logging, and human approval.
