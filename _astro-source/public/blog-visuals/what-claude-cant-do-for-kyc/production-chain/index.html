Bionic Banker visual note

KYC is an eight-step production chain

AI helps with extraction, but the bank still needs screening, review, evidence, audit, and monitoring.

Demo layer Collect and extract

Documents become fields. Useful, but only the front door.

Control layer Screen and verify

Sanctions, PEP, adverse media, authenticity, and liveness checks.

Decision layer Review and preserve

Exceptions, reviewer decisions, reason codes, and audit trail.

Live layer Monitor after onboarding

Risk changes after the account opens. The system has to keep watching.

A model can read. A compliance system has to prove.
