What Broke. What We Learned.

Each version failed differently — and each failure made the next one possible

v1 — The Brute
Single Prompt
One giant prompt, one shot
💥 What broke
Hallucinated entire transaction histories
Confidently cited nonexistent evidence
No way to verify its reasoning
💡 What we learned
A single prompt can't reason AND report
Confidence ≠ accuracy without structure
v2 — The Thinker
Structured Reasoning
Multi-step with chain-of-thought
💥 What broke
Reasoning was better but reports were worse
Analysis quality didn't transfer to output
Still one model doing two jobs
💡 What we learned
Thinking and writing are different skills
One model can't optimize for both
v3 — The Split Brain
Dual-Model
Reasoner + Synthesizer working together
💥 What still breaks
Token cost is 3x higher than v1
Reasoner/Synthesizer can disagree
💡 What worked
Separation of concerns = cleaner output
Each model excels at its specific job
Reports are finally investigation-grade
