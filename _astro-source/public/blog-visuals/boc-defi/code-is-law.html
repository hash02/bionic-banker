How Enforcement Works

Traditional Finance vs. DeFi Lending
🏦 Traditional Banking
1
Application Submitted
Paper forms, credit checks, income verification. Days to weeks.
2
Human Review
Loan officer reviews, manager approves. Judgment calls, exceptions.
3
Funds Disbursed
Money moves through clearing systems. Settlement in 1-3 days.
4
Default Handling
Collections team, negotiations, legal proceedings. Months to years.
5
Record Keeping
Internal databases. Request-only access. Retention policies vary.
⛓️ DeFi (Aave V3)
1
Wallet Connected
Connect wallet, deposit collateral. No application. Seconds.
2
Smart Contract Validates
Code checks collateral ratio. No humans. No exceptions. No judgment calls.
3
Instant Disbursement
Funds arrive in your wallet. Same block. Same second.
4
Automatic Liquidation
Health factor drops below 1? Liquidated instantly. No negotiation. No mercy.
5
Permanent Record
Every transaction on-chain forever. Public. Verifiable. Immutable.
Law is the Law
Enforced by people, regulators, courts. Flexible but slow and fallible.
Code is the Law
Enforced by mathematics and consensus. Fast, transparent, immutable.
