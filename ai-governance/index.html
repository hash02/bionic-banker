AI governance in finance is an authority problem.

Finance teams do not only need to know which model is being used. They need to know what the system can decide, which tools it can touch, what evidence it keeps, and where it must stop for human review.

Control map

Tool governance is not enough for agentic workflows.

Tool governance asks whether a model, platform, vendor, or dataset is approved. Authority governance asks what the connected workflow is allowed to influence after the tool is approved.

Vendor, data, procurement, retention, risk tier.

FAQ

Questions people usually ask first.

What is AI governance in finance?

AI governance in finance is the control layer around how AI systems are selected, used, monitored, reviewed, and limited before they affect customers, money movement, risk decisions, or regulated workflows.

What is authority governance for AI agents?

Authority governance defines what an AI agent is allowed to observe, recommend, update, trigger, or execute, and where human approval is required before an action leaves the system.

Why is agentic AI different from a chatbot?

A chatbot mostly responds. An agentic workflow can plan steps, call tools, move across systems, and trigger actions. That makes the permission boundary and evidence trail more important.

What should financial institutions record when AI influences a workflow?

They should preserve input data, source documents, model or vendor used, instruction class, tools called, output, human reviewer, approval or rejection, timestamp, escalation path, and final action.
