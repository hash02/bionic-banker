Stablecoins are becoming machine payment rails.

That does not mean autonomous finance is mature.

It means the back office problem is arriving faster.

A recent market signal from Keyrock, summarized by FinanceFeeds, says AI agents settled more than $73M across 176M onchain transactions from May 2025 to April 2026. The average payment was tiny: about $0.31 to $0.48. Most used USDC.

That is interesting, but the useful finance question is not “will agents use stablecoins?”

The useful question is:

Can anyone explain the payment after it happens?

Before AI agents can pay safely, finance teams need a control trail:

- what did the agent request?
- which rule authorized the spend?
- which wallet, token, and chain were used?
- what proof shows the payment happened?
- what proof shows the service was delivered?
- what exception path catches mismatches?
- can a reviewer replay the record without guessing?

The chain can prove value moved.

It cannot prove the agent had authority.
It cannot prove the vendor delivered.
It cannot prove the payment belongs in the ledger.

That is why the next serious layer is not just faster settlement.

It is receipts, reconciliation, delivery checks, and human review.
