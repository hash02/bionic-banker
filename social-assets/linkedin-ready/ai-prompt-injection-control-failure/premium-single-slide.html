BIONIC BANKER / AI SECURITY
AI can be hacked through instructions.
The real risk is not a clever phrase. It is untrusted text crossing into trusted instruction, tool, or approval space.
01 / UNTRUSTED
Outside content
Email, PDF, web page, retrieved note, browser text, or tool result.
02 / MODEL
Context window
The AI reads evidence and instructions in the same place unless the system separates them.
03 / GATES
Control layer
Label source, restrict tools, require approval, and keep an audit log.
04 / ACTION
Safe output
Answer, summarize, draft, or escalate — without leaking secrets or taking hidden actions.

WHAT NORMAL USERS SHOULD KNOW

Do not paste secrets into a system that cannot show what it trusted.

1
Treat outside text as data, not authority.
2
Keep passwords, keys, IDs, and private files out.
3
Never let an AI spend, send, approve, or delete by default.
bionicbanker.tech

If you cannot trace what the AI saw, trusted, called, and approved, do not trust it with sensitive work.
