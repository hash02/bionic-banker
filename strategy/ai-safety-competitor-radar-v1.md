# AI Safety / Control-Layer Competitor Radar v1

Date: 2026-06-16
Purpose: identify Bionic Banker's value, edge, shipping lane, competitor/inspiration set, and monitoring loop.

## Executive answer

This is a good direction.

Bionic Banker should not become a generic AI news page or generic AI tools directory. The stronger lane is:

> AI safety literacy + practical control maps + finance/risk proof labs.

The public promise:

> Bionic Banker helps people and finance operators use AI without blindly trusting it.

The sharper professional promise:

> Turn agent traces into gates, evidence, audit trails, and human review.

## The edge

Bionic Banker has a useful overlap most competitors do not combine well:

1. AI literacy for normal people.
2. AI governance/risk language for serious buyers.
3. Finance/AML/compliance context.
4. Local proof projects, not only opinions.
5. High-density visual packaging.
6. Public-safe boundaries: what it proves / what it does not prove.

## Competitor / inspiration groups

### 1. General AI literacy

| Name | What they do | Bionic gap |
|---|---|---|
| Elements of AI | broad beginner AI course | not finance/control-specific |
| DeepLearning.AI AI for Everyone | business AI literacy | less tactical risk workflow |
| Google AI learning resources | AI skills and learning paths | platform/vendor framing |
| Microsoft AI Business School | executive/business AI adoption | enterprise-polished, less proof-lab oriented |
| Coursera/LinkedIn/Udemy AI literacy courses | structured learning | not a fast visual/control-map content engine |

Bionic move: shorter, visual, practical, source-backed, risk-aware.

### 2. AI safety / responsible AI frameworks

| Name | What they do | Bionic gap |
|---|---|---|
| NIST AI RMF | Govern / Map / Measure / Manage AI risk | heavy framework; needs operator translation |
| NIST GenAI Profile | GenAI-specific risk actions | needs practical checklists and demos |
| OWASP LLM Top 10 | LLM app security risks | too technical for normal users |
| MITRE ATLAS | adversarial AI threat model | needs plain-language examples |
| AI Incident Database | real-world AI failures | needs control lessons and templates |
| Partnership on AI | responsible AI reports | less tactical for small teams |

Bionic move: translate frameworks into visual controls, checklists, and lab demos.

### 3. Prompt injection / hallucination education
