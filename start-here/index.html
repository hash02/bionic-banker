Clear limits

What stays outside the game.

The site stays careful. It shows judgment and system design while keeping outside-action power, private data, and regulated decisions out of scope.

  • No live trading, wallet movement, or fund transfer authority.
  • No SAR filing, KYC approval, enforcement, or final compliance power.
  • No customer, employer, private wallet, private prompt, key, token, or internal infrastructure data.
  • Portfolio grade public summary, not a commercial deployment claim.
