Representation standard

Every useful page should answer six quiet questions.

Signal

What changed, appeared, or became worth checking?

Source trail

What record, row, file, public source, or metric supports it?

System record

What did the page preserve so the reader can inspect the path?

Missing context

What is not known, not public, or not safe to infer?

Next question

What should a human reader ask before any real decision?

Limit

What authority, claim, or action is explicitly outside the page?

Clear limits

The restraint is part of the value.

The public work is intentionally careful. It shows reasoning, records, checks, and boundaries without exposing private data or claiming regulated authority.

  • No live trading, transfer, wallet-control, or fund-movement claim.
  • No copy-trading instruction or recommendation to follow a public wallet trade.
  • No SAR filing, KYC approval, enforcement, or final compliance decision.
  • No employer data, customer data, private prompts, secrets, keys, tokens, or private infrastructure details.
  • Public records only. Any real deployment needs separate governance, testing, data access, legal review, and ownership.
