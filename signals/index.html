Operating Rule

Signals are prompts for judgment

A useful signal should create a better question, not a blind action. The board is allowed to observe, compare, and explain. It is not allowed to recommend a trade or hide uncertainty.

ObserveWhat changed

Market price, heartbeat state, source-note confidence, or a source row.

ExplainWhy it matters

Connect the row to risk, workflow design, or a learning note.

BoundWhat it cannot do

No advice, no autonomous action, no performance claim.

PackageWhat comes next

Turn useful patterns into an app improvement, report, or checker.

Unblocked lane

Public wallet watch can become a report lane.

Whale-watching is a normal public-market habit: people watch known wallets, large transfers, exchange flows, and community-tracked addresses to ask better questions about market behavior. Bionic Banker can document those rows as public-address analysis while keeping the line clear: context, not copy-trading advice.

Why it belongs Whale watching is already common market behavior

Traders often watch popular public wallets, large transfers, exchange flows, and known community addresses to understand what other actors may be doing. The useful version explains the context instead of turning the movement into a command.

What the report can record Address, movement, source, context, and limit

A public-wallet report can preserve the address label source, chain, transaction link, timing, size, direction, and a plain note about why the row is interesting.

What stays blocked No copy-trade signal or external submission

The page can publish educational records. It cannot recommend trades, move funds, register accounts, submit to outside programs, or unfreeze Pancake without review.

Allowed: publish educational public-wallet reports and examples. Still blocked: Pancake, account registration, external submissions, fund movement, wallet control, and any recommendation to follow a wallet trade.

Public Wallet Watch report format.

This is the repeatable shape for a daily or biweekly public-address report. It turns whale watching into a source record: public address, public source, what moved, why people watch it, what is missing, and the AML review question it raises.

Address or public label address or label

The public wallet, entity label, or watch-list name. Use a public label/source when available.

Chain chain

The public blockchain where the movement was observed.

Public source public source

Explorer, public label page, dashboard, or community-maintained public watch list.

What moved what moved

Asset, amount band, transaction type, exchange in/out, bridge, swap, consolidation, or distribution.

Timing timing

When the row was observed and whether it relates to nearby market/news context.

Why people watch this why people watch

Why the address or behavior is notable in public-market culture.

AML review question aml review question

The human question this public row would raise inside a bounded review workflow.

Missing context missing context

What the row cannot know: intent, ownership, off-chain reason, customer identity, or full history.

Clear limit clear limit

The report is context only, not advice, accusation, or a compliance decision.
