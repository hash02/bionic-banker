What Bionic Banker is tracking

Public intelligence filter for agents, tools, AI finance workflows, and money trails. Not a news feed. The page asks which technology changes behavior, what source record shows it, and what clear limit keeps the claim honest.

Rule Every new tool has to earn a clear report.

If a technology changes how we build, review, automate, or explain finance-risk systems, it becomes a candidate for a source record, public note, clear limit, article, or system improvement. Hype is not enough. It has to help produce judgment or a better boundary.

How the research loop works

Hash brings the world

Physical-world context, finance work, public signals, user frustration, and the messy questions that matter.

Bionic Banker turns it into a map

Turn that context into sharper questions, safer boundaries, pages, checks, and small public summaries.

The site keeps score

Useful patterns become apps, reports, or deterministic gates. Weak claims stay out.

The loop improves

The private operating layer gets sharper, and the public site gets clearer records, source notes, and limits.

This is the collaboration rule: outside-world signal becomes a question, question becomes a test, test becomes a public summary only when it earns support.
