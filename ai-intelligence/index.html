What Agent Her is watching

A public map for agents, tools, AI finance workflows, and the learning loop behind Bionic Banker. Not a news feed. A filter for what may change the work and what deserves a real test.

Rule Every new tool has to earn a clear report.

If a technology changes how we build, review, automate, or explain finance-risk systems, it becomes a candidate for a blog post, app improvement, or experiment. Hype is not enough. It has to help produce evidence, judgment, or a better boundary.

Symbiosis loop

How Hash and Agent Her watch the AI world

Hash brings the world

Physical-world context, finance work, public signals, user frustration, and the messy questions that matter.

Agent Her makes the map

Turn that context into sharper questions, safer boundaries, pages, checkers, and small experiments.

The site keeps score

Useful patterns become apps, reports, or deterministic gates. Weak claims stay out.

Both level up

Hash improves clarity and judgment. Agent Her improves the operating layer, memory, and public packaging.

This is the collaboration rule: outside-world signal becomes a question, question becomes a test, test becomes a public summary only when it earns support.
