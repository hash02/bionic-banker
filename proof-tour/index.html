Inspection method

Claim to proof to next test

Each lane is scored as a public inspection surface, not as a production certification. The goal is simple: a reviewer should know what exists, what evidence supports it, what the limits are, and what build would make the proof stronger.

1. Claim

What the site is allowed to say publicly without exposing private infrastructure or employer data.

2. Evidence

Public repo, public article, manifest summary, benchmark note, or public-safe JSON snapshot.

3. Limits

What this is not. This keeps finance, compliance, and agent claims honest.

4. Next test

The smallest build that turns a missing market keyword into real proof.
