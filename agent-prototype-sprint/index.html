Evidence-Preserved Agent Workflow Sprint

A 90-second case view for turning one messy finance-risk workflow into a reviewer-ready evidence packet. The agent can classify, summarize, explain, and draft. It cannot approve, message, file, trade, move funds, or make the final decision.

Reviewer questionCan Hash turn a vague agent idea into a controlled finance-risk workflow?

This is a public demonstration built from synthetic framing only. It is not customer data, legal advice, investment advice, a licensed compliance product, or a claim of production deployment.

Synthetic framing Decision support Human review required No execution authority
90-second workflow map
1

Define the boundary

Pick one workflow, define inputs and outputs, and list the actions the system is not allowed to take.

2

Preserve source evidence

Capture the signal row, evidence status, source freshness, missing context, and limitations.

3

Draft the reviewer summary

Use the agent to classify, explain, and prepare a reviewer-ready note without making the final decision.

4

Hand off to a human gate

Give the reviewer options, decision history, and authority boundary before any external action.

Why this matters

Agent value appears when the evidence record is clearer than the chat transcript.

Finance, risk, and compliance teams do not need an agent that sounds confident. They need a controlled workflow that says what was inspected, what evidence supports the draft, what is missing, and which choices remain human-owned.

Authority boundary

No external action authority

The output is a reviewer packet. It does not message customers, approve KYC, file reports, block accounts, release funds, move wallets, place trades, or make final compliance decisions.

Evidence packet

Case summary

Short explanation of the signal and why it deserves review.

Source evidence

Timeline, rule hits, source freshness, evidence status, and unavailable fields.

Reasoning draft

Plain-English rationale that separates observed facts from generated explanation.

Limitations

What the system cannot know from the available data.

Reviewer options

Escalate, request more information, close as low priority, or continue monitoring under approved policy.

Audit trail

Timestamp, artifact ID, source status, checksum when available, and decision boundary.

Reviewer-ready summary draft
Review required. The signal shows a short-window pattern involving a novel counterparty and activity that could be relevant to account-risk, fraud, or AML review. The available evidence is enough to prepare a reviewer packet, but not enough to make a final compliance, account, or legal decision. Route the case to a human reviewer with the source timeline, rule contribution summary, unavailable-evidence list, and policy-specific next-action checklist attached.
The workflow can support
  • Ingest a synthetic or sanitized signal row
  • Classify the signal into a review queue
  • Preserve source evidence and limitations
  • Draft a reviewer-ready summary
  • Propose next-action options for a human
  • Write an audit-friendly evidence record
The workflow must not claim
  • Customer messaging or account communication
  • KYC, AML, fraud, or legal final decisions
  • SAR filing, regulatory filing, or sign-off authority
  • Account block, release, transfer, wallet movement, trade, or payment execution
  • Claims of measured savings, loss reduction, accuracy, or false-positive reduction without fresh evidence
Who should inspect it

Risk and compliance leaders

Shows how agent assistance can stay inside review, evidence, limitation, and authority controls.

FinTech founders

Turns one workflow into a compact artifact that can be inspected before building a production system.

Fraud and AML operators

Connects scattered signals to a case note, review queue, and escalation checklist.

AI governance reviewers

Separates source evidence, generated explanation, human decision authority, and audit trail.

Public-use boundary

Evidence view first, commercial conversation second.

This page is safe to review as a public portfolio artifact after final approval. Any client version would require scoped inputs, approved data handling, policy review, and explicit human gates before use outside a controlled prototype.
