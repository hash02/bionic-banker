Open Intelligence
Editorial rules

What the editor checks.

Lead with reader value before internal process.

Use questions before claims when a topic is complex.

Show existing proof before future direction.

Attach numbers to source context and limits.

Use plain definitions for new AI terms.

Keep sensitive operational details, credentials, and employer or client data out of the public page.

Review rubric

Six checks before a page earns attention.

Clarity

Can a first-time reader understand the point without knowing the internal system?

Evidence

Does the page link to proof, source data, reports, screenshots, or a public artifact?

Reader value

Does the page help the reader learn, inspect, compare, refresh, or decide what to open next?

Privacy

Does the page avoid credentials, sensitive system identifiers, client data, and employer data?

Overclaim

Are limitations visible wherever numbers, automation, AI, finance, or agent claims appear?

Market usefulness

Does the page map to a real question people ask about AI systems, finance automation, evidence, or deployment?
