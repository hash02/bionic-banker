# LinkedIn Attention Playbook — Bionic Banker

Date: 2026-06-18

## Goal

Increase attention on Himanshu/Bionic Banker posts without spam, fake engagement, engagement pods, or low-quality growth hacks.

The strategy is to optimize for LinkedIn feed signals that generally matter: relevance, early meaningful comments, dwell time, saves/shares, profile credibility, and creator consistency.

## Positioning rule

Bionic posts should not sound like generic AI governance commentary.

Use this shape:

```text
recognizable mistake -> control model -> practical checklist -> practitioner question
```

Examples:

- Finance teams will trust AI summaries too early.
- AI agents will not be trusted because they sound confident. They will be trusted when their actions leave evidence.
- The risk is not that AI writes a bad paragraph. The risk is that the paragraph becomes a decision record.

## Best post format for the next 30 days

Use one of three formats:

1. **Sharp single-image post**
   - 2160x2700 portrait image.
   - Big headline, one mental model, 3–5 gates.
   - Caption under 1,300 characters when possible.
   - Article link in first comment, not body.

2. **Text-only provocation**
   - No link.
   - One strong thesis.
   - Short lines.
   - End with a practitioner question.

3. **Document/carousel only when sequential**
   - Use only when the argument has steps.
   - Each slide one claim.
   - Source/boundary slide included.

## Posting rhythm

Do not post daily if quality or interaction is low.

Recommended cadence:

```text
3 posts/week
2 comment-first sessions/week
1 article/week
```

Suggested windows to test:

- Tue/Wed/Thu morning local time.
- Avoid posting immediately after another low-engagement post.
- Keep at least 24h between major Bionic posts.

## 45-minute activation routine

### 20 minutes before posting

Comment thoughtfully on 5–8 relevant posts from:

- AI governance people
- model risk / audit / compliance people
- fintech operators
- AML / fraud / risk professionals
- enterprise AI builders

Rules:

- No generic praise.
- Add one useful point or question.
- Do not drop links.

### Post

Use:

```text
image + short caption + practitioner question
```

Put the article link in first comment.

### 30 minutes after posting

- Reply quickly to every comment.
- Add one clarifying comment yourself after 10–15 minutes.
- Send the post to 2–3 relevant people only if it genuinely matches their work. No mass DM.

## Hooks that fit Bionic

- An AI answer is not evidence.
- The dangerous AI output is not the weird one. It is the confident one.
- Finance teams do not need more AI confidence. They need source trails.
- A prompt is not a control.
- If an AI agent can act, the audit trail becomes the product.
- The question is not “can the model do it?” The question is “can the team prove what happened?”

## Experiments

Run A/B by topic shape, not by random tweaks.

### Experiment A: Human mistake vs abstract concept

- A1: “AI hallucination evidence checklist”
- A2: “Finance teams will trust confident AI summaries too early”

Prediction: A2 performs better.

### Experiment B: Image vs text-only

Use the same thesis once as sharp image, once as text-only a week later.

### Experiment C: Question type

- Generic: “What do you think?”
- Practitioner: “What AI-generated output would you never let through without a human evidence check?”

Prediction: practitioner question performs better.

## What not to do

- Do not join fake engagement pods.
- Do not tag many people who are not directly relevant.
- Do not post external links in the main body if reach matters.
- Do not overpost low-engagement variations of the same idea.
- Do not make every post a Bionic article announcement.
