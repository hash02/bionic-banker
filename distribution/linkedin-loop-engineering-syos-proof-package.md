I am looking for people working on agent loops, memory, evals, drift detection, or human-in-the-loop controls.

I have been digging through my old SYOS work and found a clearer thread than I expected.

Jun 2025: SYOS and SYOS-System public repos.

At the time, I did not have the clean language for it.

Now I would call it loop engineering.

My working thesis:

Prompting is not enough. The loop around the model matters more: state, memory, evals, drift checks, traces, and human boundaries.

This matters especially in finance, where an AI output needs evidence and review before it can be trusted.

I am not claiming I was first. I am saying I have a build trail, and I want to find people working on the same edge.
