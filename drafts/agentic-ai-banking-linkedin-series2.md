Last week I published The AI Agent Gap. $67 billion market. Only 21% in production.

The response was clear: "where's the proof?"

So I pulled real production data from 4 countries. Not vendor slides. Not hype. Actual numbers from banks that are shipping autonomous agents right now.

Here's what I found. Part 2 of the Agentic AI in Banking series.

US: @JPMorgan reads 12,000 credit agreements in seconds. 360,000 legal hours saved per year. 500+ AI use cases live in production. AML false positives down 95%.

@WellsFargo hit 200 million fully autonomous customer interactions. @BNYMellon built Eliza, a platform where employees design their own AI agents. 150 AI-powered offerings planned.

India: @HDFCBank deployed Eva to 127,000 rural entrepreneurs. Instant loans. No branch. No call center. Responds in 0.4 seconds. They asked "how do we reach people we can't reach" before "how do we go faster."

Australia: @ANZ_AU collapsed 20 legacy platforms into 1 AI-powered dashboard. First bank in APAC to deploy Salesforce Agentforce at scale. Saved bankers ~1 month per year. And they're hiring 50% more bankers while doing it.

Canada: The numbers are strong.
RBC #3 globally (Evident AI Index, 4th year). Aiden: 1 analyst covers 50 companies (was 15). $1B AI target by 2027.
Scotiabank AIDox: 90% of commercial emails handled autonomously. 70% team redeployed.
CIBC: 1.2 million hours saved Q1 2026. 30K active users. Best GenAI Initiative, two years running.

The gap is not invention. It's scale. US has 500 use cases in production. Canada has world-class pilots. 99% of banks plan to deploy agents. Only 11% have.

2026 is the window. Nobody owns agentic AI yet. Build now, lead later.
