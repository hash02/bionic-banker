99% of banks plan to deploy autonomous AI agents.

Only 11% have actually done it.

I looked at the real numbers. Not hype. Not press releases. Actual production data from banks across 4 countries.

Here's what I found:

US: @JPMorgan's COiN reads 12,000 credit agreements in seconds. Saves 360,000 legal hours/year. They have 500+ AI use cases in production. @WellsFargo's assistant hit 200 million autonomous interactions. @BNYMellon lets employees build their own agents.

India: @HDFCBank's Eva responds in 0.4 seconds. Deployed to 127,000 rural entrepreneurs. Instant loan applications through AI, no branch needed.

Australia: @ANZ_AU deployed Salesforce Agentforce at scale. Collapsed 20 platforms into 1 dashboard. Saved bankers ~1 month/year. First in APAC.

Canada: Strong pilots, real wins.
@RBC is #3 globally in the Evident AI Index. Aiden platform: 1 analyst covers 50 companies (was 15). Targeting $1B in AI value by 2027.
@Scotiabank's AIDox handles 90% of 1,500 daily commercial emails autonomously. 70% of team redeployed.
@CIBC saved 1.2 million hours in Q1 2026 alone. 30,000 active users. Won Best GenAI Initiative twice.

The gap? Not invention. Scale and speed. US has 500 use cases in production. Canada has world-class pilots.

But here's the thing: agentic AI is so new that nobody owns it yet. 2026 is the window. Build now, lead later.

The agents are coming. The question for Canadian banking is whether we build them or optimize them after someone else does.
