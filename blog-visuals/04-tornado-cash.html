Tornado Cash: The Mixer My Engine Watches — and why it matters

How a Crypto Mixer Works
1. Deposit
User deposits ETH into a pool (0.1, 1, 10, or 100 ETH)
2. Mix
Smart contract pools funds with others — breaks the on-chain link between sender and receiver
3. Withdraw
User withdraws to a new address using a cryptographic proof — no visible connection
4. The Problem
$7B+ processed — used by both privacy seekers AND criminals (Lazarus Group, ransomware, exploits)
5. What My Engine Catches
Zero-value probes, structuring patterns, repeated contract calls — the behavior before the crime
