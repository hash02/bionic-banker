Boltzmann Machine
Geoffrey Hinton adds temperature. Networks can now escape local minima traps through simulated annealing.
Thermodynamics to Training
2006
Deep Learning Revolution
Hinton shows deep networks can be trained layer by layer. The AI winter ends. Energy minimization at scale.

2017-2023
